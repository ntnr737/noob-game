Brand Story

Crunchiko: Crunch Big. Live Light.

Crunchiko combines "Crunch" (joyful texture) and "iko" (modern, global, memorable sound) to build a fresh Indian healthy-snack identity.

Our mission is simple: make traditional makhana exciting for modern consumers using clean ingredients, smart roasting, and bold flavors.

Brand Snapshot

Tone: Fun, premium, trustworthy

Promise: Better-for-you crunch without compromise
